Sanchr 1.0.0 — internal release.

End-to-end encrypted one-to-one messaging between Android and iOS, built on
libsignal with sealed sender. Voice and video calls. Photos, videos,
documents, voice notes and stickers, with a photo editor and an in-app
document viewer.

App Lock and screenshot protection. Crash reporting is off unless you turn
it on. Delete Account wipes every key on the device.

Internal-track preview: please report issues with your device model and
Android version.
