Sanchr 1.0.0 — internal release.

Android↔iOS end-to-end encrypted 1:1 text messaging, built on libsignal +
sealed sender. Phone-number registration, SMS OTP, new-chat lookup,
reactive logout with full local-key wipe.

This build is an internal-track preview for testing only. Expect short
sessions and occasional restarts; please report issues with your device
model, Android version, and a reproduction timeline.
