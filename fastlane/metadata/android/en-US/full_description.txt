Sanchr is a messaging app for people who would rather their conversations stayed between the people in them.

Messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted using the Signal protocol (libsignal). Encryption is not a mode you switch on — there is no way to send an unencrypted message.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Message one to one, with replies, reactions and forwarding
• Voice and video calls
• Send photos, videos, documents, voice notes and stickers
• Mark up a photo before you send it — crop, rotate, draw
• Send a photo that can be opened once and then is gone
• Search your conversations
• Keep sensitive media in a separate encrypted vault

PRIVACY BY CONSTRUCTION

• Sealed sender: the server does not learn who is talking to whom
• Documents, PDFs and images open inside Sanchr, so a decrypted file is not handed to another app just to be read
• App Lock, with biometrics if you want them
• Screenshot protection
• Crash reporting is off unless you turn it on, carries no user identifier, and goes to infrastructure we run rather than a third party
• Delete Account removes your account and wipes every key on the device

WHAT WE DO NOT DO

• No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
• No selling or sharing of your data
• No reading of your messages — we cannot, and neither can anyone who compels us

A NOTE ON HONESTY

End-to-end encryption protects the contents of your conversations. It does not make you anonymous, and it cannot protect a device that someone else controls. Anyone who tells you their app makes you untraceable is selling something.

Sanchr requires a phone number to register, and that number is how other people find you.

OPEN

Sanchr's Android and iOS clients are open source. If a claim on this page matters to you, you are welcome to go and check it.
